Zomato Data Exploration & Business Analytics using SQL

Overview

Analyzed 9,000+ restaurant records using Microsoft SQL Server to uncover business insights related to restaurant distribution, customer ratings, pricing, cuisines, and online delivery across countries and Indian cities.

Tech Stack

SQL: Microsoft SQL Server, Joins, CTEs, Views, Window Functions, Aggregations, Data Cleaning

Key Work

• Cleaned and validated data by identifying duplicate restaurant IDs, correcting city names, and removing unnecessary columns.

• Combined datasets using primary-key relationships to enrich records with country-level information.

• Used CTEs, views, and window functions to analyze restaurant distribution and calculate country-wise percentages.

• Evaluated ratings, votes, pricing, table booking, and online delivery to identify business patterns.

• Analyzed city- and locality-level restaurant concentration, cuisine preferences, and service availability.

Business Insights

• India accounted for approximately 90.67% of the dataset's restaurant records.

• Online delivery availability was concentrated in India and the UAE among the countries analyzed.

• Connaught Place, New Delhi, had the highest restaurant concentration in the analyzed dataset, with 122 listings.

• In Connaught Place, restaurants offering table booking had an average rating of 3.9 compared with 3.7 for those without it.

• Identified restaurants matching defined rating, pricing, cuisine, and service criteria to support data-driven comparisons.

Objective

Demonstrate practical SQL-based data preparation, validation, exploratory analysis, and business insight generation to support data-informed decision-making.
